NeoAPRS

APRS client for Windows, macOS, Linux and the Raspberry Pi

See who is on the air around you, chat with other hams and know your message arrived. When the internet or the local network goes down, keep talking: the built-in Mercury modem carries your messages far beyond VHF range. Works with the radio you already have, and it is free.

How you use it
  1. Download for WindowsNeoAPRS-Setup.exe · no administrator rights needed
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Windows says Windows protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Go through the installer. On its last page, Open NeoAPRS now starts it. NeoAPRS asks for your call, your position and your radio. No radio at hand: choose the radio type Dummy.
  1. Download for MacNeoAPRS-arm64.dmg · Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14 or newer
  2. Open the downloaded file and drag NeoAPRS to Applications.
  3. The first time, right-click NeoAPRS in Applications and choose Open. If the Mac says it could not be verified: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway.
  4. NeoAPRS asks for your call, your position and your radio. No radio at hand: choose the radio type Dummy.
  1. Download for LinuxNeoAPRS-x86_64.AppImage · Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 35 and newer
  2. Open a terminal and make it a program.
    chmod +x ~/Downloads/NeoAPRS-x86_64.AppImage
  3. Start it. From then on a double-click on the file starts it too.
    ~/Downloads/NeoAPRS-x86_64.AppImage
  4. NeoAPRS asks for your call, your position and your radio. No radio at hand: choose the radio type Dummy.
  1. Download for Raspberry Pineoaprs_arm64.deb · 64-bit Raspberry Pi OS
  2. Open a terminal and install it. It asks for your password.
    sudo apt install ~/Downloads/neoaprs_arm64.deb
  3. Start NeoAPRS, now and every time the computer starts.
    neoaprs service install --linger
  4. Open http://localhost:7303 in the browser. NeoAPRS asks for your call, your position and your radio. No radio at hand: choose the radio type Dummy.
  1. From another computer, log in to the Linux PC with SSH, the way you set it up (ssh YOU@NAME.local).
  2. Download NeoAPRS.
    wget https://github.com/oh2gba/neoaprs/releases/latest/download/neoaprs_amd64.deb
  3. Install it. It asks for your password.
    sudo apt install ./neoaprs_amd64.deb
  4. Let other computers and phones open it.
    neoaprs db set web/network true
  5. Start NeoAPRS, now and every time the computer starts.
    neoaprs service install --linger
  6. Only if you have turned on a firewall: let the page through. Raspberry Pi OS and Ubuntu come with it off, so most can skip this.
    sudo ufw allow 7303/tcp
  7. Show the computer's name and its address, and the key the other computer or phone will ask for.
    hostname; hostname -I; neoaprs db get web/key
  8. On the other computer or phone, open http://NAME.local:7303 with the name in place of NAME. If it does not open, use the address instead, the first number, such as http://192.168.1.20:7303. New

APRS over HF, with Mercury

Read the first test on the air: 40 km on 10 W, every message delivered →

How it works, and what was measured: the technical note →

Mercury is a modern HF modem by Rhizomatica. NeoAPRS puts APRS on it, as a second radio beside the FM one: an SSB radio with a sound card. HF stations are on the same map and in the same messages, tagged HF.

Old HF packet, 300 baudMercury in NeoAPRS
On a noisy bandLost as soon as the noise comes upStill heard when the signal is no stronger than the noise every frame at 3 dB signal-to-noise in our simulations
A bad momentOne wrong bit and the whole packet is goneSmall errors are repaired on arrival a strong error-correcting code (LDPC)
Fading and echoesGarble the packetTaken in its stride nine carriers in 500 Hz, each with a guard time against the ionosphere's echoes
Other stationsSends on top of themListens first and waits for a free channel

Ready when the internet is not

When the internet and the phone network are down, APRS over HF still works: positions, short messages and bulletins between stations hundreds or thousands of kilometres apart. A radio, a laptop or a Raspberry Pi, a battery and a wire antenna are enough. No servers, no accounts, nothing in between.

VARA or Mercury?

What you want to doVARA, with Winlink, VarAC or BPQMercury in NeoAPRS
See who is on the air, on a mapNoYes, every station heard, with its position
Reach every station at onceMostly one station at a timeYes: beacons, bulletins and objects everyone hears
Short messages to one stationYes, in a connected chatYes, acknowledged, with no connection to set up
E-mail to and from the internetYes, through WinlinkNo
Long texts and filesYesNo, APRS messages are short

VARA means here the original VARA HF and FM and compatible modems such as xara. Both kinds of modem can live on the same radio.

  • A first

    APRS has not been carried over Mercury before. NeoAPRS does it, with the APRS you know: positions, objects and messages.

  • Far

    Stations 1000 to 2000 km away, on one HF frequency, with no digipeaters and no internet.

  • Nothing to install

    Mercury is inside NeoAPRS. The sound card and the keying are preselected; Tune and Test set up the radio.

  • Fast enough

    A frame takes 3.8 s on the air.

  • Try it first

    The Dummy HF radio makes up distant stations and plays the modem through your speakers.

Features

  1. Stations

    Every station heard, with distance, bearing and when, tagged FM or HF. The row menu sends a message or an APRS query.

  2. Messages

    Acks and retries, and the way each message went through the digipeaters. Bulletins, group messages and email through the APRS gateway.

  3. Weather

    Readings of APRS weather stations, with 24 hour and 7 day graphs.

  4. Nearby

    Digipeaters, iGates and voice frequencies, nearest first, with a digipeater's range on the map.

  5. Beacon

    Fixed, or from a GPS with SmartBeaconing. Receive only is one tick.

  6. Objects

    Your own APRS objects and items on the map, sent on FM, HF or both.

  7. Packets

    Every frame with its radio and path. Optional fill-in digipeater and receive-only iGate.

  8. Phone and tablet

    Open it on any device at home with an access key. Eight languages.
