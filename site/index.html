NeoAPRS

APRS client for Windows, macOS, Linux and the Raspberry Pi

See who is on the air around you, chat with other hams and know your message arrived. Use every radio you have at once. No internet needed, and it is free.

Your computer
How you use it
  1. Download for WindowsNeoAPRS-Setup.exe · no administrator rights needed
  2. Open the downloaded file. If Windows says Windows protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Go through the installer. On its last page, Open NeoAPRS now starts it. NeoAPRS asks for your call, your position and your radio. No radio at hand: choose a Dummy radio.
  1. Download for MacNeoAPRS-arm64.dmg · Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 14 or newer
  2. Open the downloaded file and drag NeoAPRS to Applications.
  3. The first time, right-click NeoAPRS in Applications and choose Open. If the Mac says it could not be verified: System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway.
  4. NeoAPRS asks for your call, your position and your radio. No radio at hand: choose a Dummy radio.
  1. Download for LinuxNeoAPRS-x86_64.AppImage · Ubuntu 22.04, Debian 12, Fedora 36 and newer
  2. Open a terminal and make it a program.
    chmod +x ~/Downloads/NeoAPRS-x86_64.AppImage
  3. Start it.
    ~/Downloads/NeoAPRS-x86_64.AppImage
  4. NeoAPRS asks for your call, your position and your radio. No radio at hand: choose a Dummy radio.
  1. Download for Raspberry Pineoaprs_arm64.deb · 64-bit Raspberry Pi OS
  2. Open a terminal and install it. It asks for your password.
    sudo apt install ~/Downloads/neoaprs_arm64.deb
  3. Start NeoAPRS, now and every time the computer starts.
    neoaprs service install --linger
  4. Open http://localhost:7303 in the browser. NeoAPRS asks for your call, your position and your radio. No radio at hand: choose a Dummy radio.
  1. From another computer, log in to the Linux PC with SSH, the way you set it up (ssh YOU@NAME.local).
  2. Download NeoAPRS.
    wget https://github.com/oh2gba/neoaprs/releases/latest/download/neoaprs_amd64.deb
  3. Install it. It asks for your password.
    sudo apt install ./neoaprs_amd64.deb
  4. Let other computers and phones open it.
    neoaprs db set web/network true
  5. Start NeoAPRS, now and every time the computer starts.
    neoaprs service install --linger
  6. Only if you have turned on a firewall: let the page through. Raspberry Pi OS and Ubuntu come with it off, so most can skip this.
    sudo ufw allow 7303/tcp
  7. Show the computer's name and its address, and the key the other computer or phone will ask for.
    hostname; hostname -I; neoaprs db get web/key
  8. On the other computer or phone, open http://NAME.local:7303 with the name in place of NAME. If it does not open, use the address instead, the first number, such as http://192.168.1.20:7303. Type the key. NeoAPRS asks for your call, your position and your radio. No radio at hand: choose a Dummy radio.

New

APRS over HF, with Mercury

Mercury is a modern HF modem by Rhizomatica. NeoAPRS is the first to put APRS on it.

  • Below the noise

    In the first test, a message sent at 1 W was decoded 3 dB below the noise.

  • Emergency communications

    Positions, messages and bulletins from station to station, with no internet and no digipeaters.

  • Built in

    An SSB radio and a sound card are enough. Nothing else to install.

Features

  1. Stations

    Every station heard, with distance, bearing and the radio that heard it.

  2. Messages

    Acks, retries and the path each message took. Bulletins, group messages, email and services such as APRS Thursday.

  3. Weather

    Weather stations, with 24 hour and 7 day graphs.

  4. Nearby

    Digipeaters, iGates and voice frequencies, nearest first.

  5. Layers on the map

    Aircraft, ships, tracks, the locator grid, and day and night.

  6. Beacon

    Fixed, or from a GPS with SmartBeaconing.

  7. Objects

    Your own objects and items on the map.

  8. Packets

    Every frame with its radio and path. Fill-in digipeater and receive-only iGate.

  9. Your radios

    As many as you have: Packet, Mercury and LoRa side by side.

  10. HF tests and the waterfall

    Plan a test on HF and see who else will. The channel's waterfall.

  11. Phone and tablet

    Open it on any device at home. Nine languages.
